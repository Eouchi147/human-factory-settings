# Weight management · the deep guide

Sep 30, 2026 · @Sam

A Human Factory Settings guide: what actually moves body weight and keeps it there, graded by the strength of the evidence. General education, not personal medical advice.

## At a glance

Weight follows energy in and energy out, but the body pushes back after a loss. What works is what you can keep doing: a moderate deficit, enough protein, strength training, sleep, fewer ultra-processed foods and, for obesity, medicines or surgery with a doctor.

| What moves weight | What the evidence shows | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A calorie deficit you can keep | Every named diet works about equally at 12 months; sticking to it predicts loss (r = 0.60), the diet's type does not (r = 0.07) | Strong |
| Fewer ultra-processed foods | People ate 508 kcal a day more on an ultra-processed diet than on an unprocessed one, in a controlled ward trial | Good |
| Enough protein while losing | Higher-protein diets lost 0.87 kg more fat and kept 0.43 kg more lean mass (24 trials) | Good |
| Strength training | Adds little loss but protects muscle: lean mass fell 2% with weights vs 5% with aerobic exercise alone, during the same diet | Good |
| Moving more | Exercise alone takes off 1.4 to 2.1 kg in 6 months; it matters more for keeping weight off and for health | Strong |
| Sleep | Short sleep adds about 385 kcal a day; sleeping 1.2 hours longer cut intake by about 270 kcal a day | Some |
| Weighing yourself | Programmes that include it lose about 1.7 kg more than comparison groups; alone, the effect is small. Weekly works as well as daily | Some |
| Medicines, for obesity | Semaglutide: 14.9% loss vs 2.4% on placebo; tirzepatide: 20.9% vs 3.1%. Most of it returns after stopping | Strong |
| Surgery, for severe obesity | Gastric bypass: 25% lower weight at 10 years, and fewer deaths | Good |

**What does not work:** detox teas (laxatives: the loss is water), crunches for belly fat (no fat change), fat-burner pills (0.7 to 1.1 kg at best, some linked to harm), and meal-timing tricks alone (no better than the same calories eaten any time).

If food, weight or exercise start to feel out of control, go straight to Safety, near the end.

## How your weight is set

Your weight settles where the energy you eat matches the energy you use. Resting needs take the biggest share, everyday movement varies the most, and after a loss the body pushes back for more than a year.

| Where a day's energy goes | Share | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resting: keeping organs, brain and temperature running | 60 to 70% | Set mostly by lean mass |
| Digesting food | About 10% | Higher for protein than for fat |
| Movement: exercise plus everything else you do standing and walking | 15% (sedentary) to 50% (very active) | The part that varies most between people |

Source: [National Academies, energy requirements, 2023](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/26818/chapter/6).

**Everyday movement is a hidden dial.** Sixteen lean adults ate 1,000 kcal a day extra for 8 weeks. Two-thirds of the rise in their energy use came from non-exercise movement (fidgeting, standing, walking), and it explained a tenfold spread in how much fat they gained ([Levine 1999](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.283.5399.212)). In another study, people with obesity sat about 2 hours a day longer than lean people ([Levine 2005](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1106561)).

**Metabolism does not crash at 30 or 40.** Measured in thousands of people aged 8 days to 95 years, energy use adjusted for lean mass stays stable from about 20 to 60, then declines ([Pontzer 2021](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe5017)).

**The pushback after a loss.** Fifty adults lost 13.5 kg on a 10-week very-low-energy diet. A year later their hunger hormones had still not returned to baseline: leptin was lower, ghrelin higher, and they felt hungrier ([Sumithran 2011](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1105816)). Energy use also drops a little more than the lost weight explains: about 72 to 108 kcal a day in a controlled trial ([Müller 2015](https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.115.109173)), and 499 kcal a day in 14 people six years after the extreme losses of The Biggest Loser ([Fothergill 2016](https://doi.org/10.1002/oby.21538)). A review of 33 studies found the effect smaller in well-designed studies and fading once weight is stable ([Nunes 2022](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114521001094)).

The practical meaning: losing weight is a physics problem, keeping it off is a biology problem. Plan for the second from day one.

## Measuring what matters

BMI is a quick screen, not a diagnosis. Add your waist: keep it under half your height.

| BMI (weight in kg ÷ height in m²) | Category | Lower cut-offs for South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African and African-Caribbean backgrounds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 18.5 | Underweight | Same |
| 18.5 to 24.9 | Healthy | 18.5 to 22.9 |
| 25 to 29.9 | Overweight | 23 to 27.4 |
| 30 and over | Obesity | 27.5 and over |

Sources: [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/adult-calculator/bmi-categories.html), [NICE NG246](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng246).

| Waist ÷ height | Meaning (NICE NG246) |
| --- | --- |
| 0.40 to 0.49 | Healthy |
| 0.50 to 0.59 | Increased health risk |
| 0.60 and over | High health risk |

**Why BMI is not enough.** A 2025 Lancet Commission of 58 experts defined obesity by excess body fat and its effects, not weight alone ([Rubino 2025](<https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(24)00316-4>)). Excess fat should be confirmed with a waist measure or a direct fat measurement, except above a BMI of 40. *Clinical obesity* means the extra fat is already harming organs or daily function; *preclinical obesity* means risk is raised but function is still normal.

**Weighing yourself.** Weekly weighing works as well as daily ([Madigan 2015](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12966-015-0267-4)). Day-to-day weight moves with water and food in the gut, so judge the trend across weeks, never one morning.



## Eating to lose

A steady calorie deficit is the lever. Which diet delivers it matters far less than whether you can keep it up, as trials comparing diets head to head show again and again.

| Head-to-head test | Result | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Healthy low-fat vs healthy low-carb, 609 adults, 12 months | -5.3 vs -6.0 kg, no real difference; genes and insulin did not predict who did better | [DIETFITS, Gardner 2018](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2018.0245) |
| Atkins, Zone, Weight Watchers, Ornish, 160 adults, 1 year | -2.1 to -3.3 kg; how well people stuck to it predicted loss, the diet did not | [Dansinger 2005](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.293.1.43) |
| 48 trials of named diets vs no diet, 7,286 people | Low-carb -7.25 kg, low-fat -7.27 kg at 12 months | [Johnston 2014](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2014.10397) |
| Eating within 8 hours plus a calorie limit vs the calorie limit alone, 139 adults, 12 months | -8.0 vs -6.3 kg, difference not significant | [Liu 2022](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2114833) |
| Intermittent fasting vs ordinary dietary advice, 21 trials | No meaningful difference | [Cochrane 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD015610.pub2) |

**Protein.** While losing weight, higher-protein diets lost 0.87 kg more fat and kept 0.43 kg more lean mass across 24 trials ([Wycherley 2012](https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.112.044321)). A practical range is 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg of body weight a day, the figure in the [US Dietary Guidelines 2025 to 2030](https://cdn.realfood.gov/DGA.pdf), spread as roughly 25 to 30 g per meal ([Leidy 2015](https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.114.084038)). People with kidney disease should ask their doctor first.

**Pace.** Aim for 0.5 to 1 kg a week ([NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-weight/managing-your-weight/tips-to-help-you-lose-weight/), [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/losing-weight/index.html)); 5% over six months is a good first goal ([NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/adult-overweight-obesity/treatment)).

**Why 5% is worth it.** In 3,234 adults with prediabetes, a programme aiming for 7% loss and 150 minutes of activity a week cut new diabetes by 58% over 2.8 years; the drug metformin cut it by 31% ([Diabetes Prevention Program 2002](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa012512)).

**What to eat.** Mostly whole and minimally processed foods, plenty of fibre, few sugary drinks. The why and the numbers are in the Supplements and nutrition guide.

## Moving

Exercise alone takes off little weight, because people eat back part of what they burn. Its real jobs are protecting muscle, helping keep weight off, and improving health at any weight.

| Finding | Numbers | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exercise alone, 411 women, 6 months | -1.4 to -2.1 kg; the highest dose lost about half the predicted amount | [Church 2009](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0004515) |
| How much activity | 150 to 250 minutes a week prevents gain and adds modest loss; above 250 minutes a week, loss becomes clinically meaningful | [ACSM 2009](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0b013e3181949333) |
| Weights during a diet, 160 older adults with obesity | Weight -9% in every exercise group; lean mass -2% with resistance training, -3% combined, -5% aerobic only | [Villareal 2017](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1616338) |
| Sitting | Adopting lean people's standing and walking habits could burn about 350 kcal a day more | [Levine 2005](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1106561) |

**The plan in one line:** strength training two or three times a week to keep muscle, walking every day, and less sitting between.

## Sleep, sitting and the food around you

Three modern changes push weight up, and each has a measured effect: ultra-processed food, short sleep, and long hours sitting.

| Change | What was measured | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ultra-processed food | 20 adults on a hospital ward ate as much as they wanted of two diets matched for calories on the plate, sugar, salt and fibre. On the ultra-processed diet they ate 508 kcal a day more and gained 0.9 kg in 2 weeks; on the unprocessed diet they lost 0.9 kg | [Hall 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2019.05.008) |
| Short sleep | After partial sleep loss, people ate about 385 kcal a day more, with no change in energy used (11 studies) | [Al Khatib 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/ejcn.2016.201) |
| More sleep | 80 adults who slept under 6.5 hours added 1.2 hours a night and ate about 270 kcal a day less; their weight fell | [Tasali 2022](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2021.8098) |
| Sitting | People with obesity sat about 2 hours a day longer than lean people, and the habit did not change when their weight did | [Levine 2005](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1106561) |

The honest limit: the ultra-processed trial had 20 people at one site, and the sleep studies are small. The direction is consistent; the exact sizes will move as bigger trials report.

## Keeping it off

Most people regain much of what they lose, but not all: five years after structured programmes the average person still kept more than 3 kg off, and about 1 in 5 people with overweight keep off 10% for at least a year.

| Finding | Numbers | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long-term results of 29 programmes | More than 3 kg kept off on average after 5 years | [Anderson 2001](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/74.5.579) |
| How many succeed | About 20% lose 10% and keep it off a year or more; the odds improve after 2 to 5 years of keeping it off | [Wing and Phelan 2005](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/82.1.222S) |
| After weight-loss medicine stops | Two-thirds of the loss returned within a year (11.6 of 17.3 percentage points) | [STEP 1 extension](https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.14725) |

**What people who keep it off do.** Members of the US National Weight Control Registry, who lost an average of 33 kg and kept it off for more than 5 years, report the same habits ([Wing and Phelan 2005](https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/82.1.222S)):

1. About an hour of activity a day.
2. A lower-calorie diet they can live with.
3. Breakfast most days.
4. Weighing themselves regularly.
5. Eating the same way on weekdays and weekends.

This is observational: people who volunteer for a registry may differ from everyone else. It is still the best map we have of what long-term success looks like.

**Why regain happens:** hunger hormones stay shifted for over a year after a loss (see How your weight is set). Plan maintenance as its own phase, with the same protein, strength training and sleep that got you there.

## Medicines

For obesity, the newest medicines produce average losses of 11 to 21% in trials, far more than diet alone, but most of the weight returns when they stop. A doctor prescribes them, with a plan for food, strength training and what happens next.

| Medicine | Taken | Trial | Weight change vs placebo | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Semaglutide 2.4 mg (Wegovy) | Weekly injection | [STEP 1](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183), 1,961 adults | -14.9% vs -2.4% | 68 weeks |
| Tirzepatide 15 mg (Mounjaro, Zepbound) | Weekly injection | [SURMOUNT-1](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038), 2,539 adults | -20.9% vs -3.1% | 72 weeks |
| Tirzepatide vs semaglutide, head to head | Weekly injections | [SURMOUNT-5](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2416394), 751 adults, open label | -20.2% vs -13.7% | 72 weeks |
| Semaglutide 25 mg (Wegovy pill) | Daily tablet | [OASIS 4](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2500969), 307 adults | -13.6% vs -2.2% | 64 weeks |
| Orforglipron (Foundayo) | Daily tablet | [ATTAIN-1](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2511774), 3,127 adults, top trial dose | -11.2% vs -2.1% | 72 weeks |

**Heart protection.** In 17,604 adults with heart disease and overweight but no diabetes, semaglutide cut major heart attacks, strokes and cardiovascular deaths by 20% (HR 0.80) over about 40 months ([SELECT](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2307563)).

**After stopping.** One year after semaglutide and lifestyle support stopped, people had regained two-thirds of what they lost ([STEP 1 extension](https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.14725)). Treat it as long-term care, like blood pressure medicine, unless your doctor plans otherwise.

**Muscle.** With tirzepatide, about 75% of the weight lost was fat and 25% lean tissue, the same split as with diet on placebo ([SURMOUNT-1 body scans](https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.16275)). Protein and strength training protect muscle, as a 2025 joint advisory on nutrition with these drugs stresses ([joint advisory, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2025.100181)).

**Side effects** ([EU product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wegovy-epar-product-information_en.pdf)): very common: nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, constipation, stomach pain. Common: gallstones, headache, dizziness. Rare but serious: pancreatitis. Very rare: a sudden loss of vision in one eye (NAION), up to 1 in 10,000 people ([EMA 2025](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/news/prac-concludes-eye-condition-naion-very-rare-side-effect-semaglutide-medicines-ozempic-rybelsus-wegovy)). Stop at least 2 months before a planned pregnancy. In January 2026 the FDA found no rise in suicidal thoughts across 91 trials and asked to remove that warning; report any new low mood anyway ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-requests-removal-suicidal-behavior-and-ideation-warning-glucagon-peptide-1-receptor-agonist-glp)).

**Who they are for** (EU and Canadian labels): adults with a BMI of 30 or more, or 27 or more with a weight-related condition, alongside diet and activity. France's medicines agency is explicit: not for cosmetic weight loss ([ANSM](https://ansm.sante.fr/actualites/analogues-du-glp-1-indiques-dans-le-traitement-de-lobesite-lansm-fait-evoluer-leurs-conditions-de-prescription-et-de-delivrance)). WHO issued its first guideline on these medicines on 1 December 2025, with conditional support for long-term use in adults ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news/item/01-12-2025-who-issues-global-guideline-on-the-use-of-glp-1-medicines-in-treating-obesity)).

| Where | Status, September 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| France | Wegovy and Mounjaro reimbursed since 15 June 2026 for BMI 40 or more, or 35 or more with a weight-related condition, after nutritional care has not worked; the first prescription comes from a specialist ([ameli](https://www.ameli.fr/assure/actualites/obesite-de-nouveaux-medicaments-peuvent-etre-pris-en-charge-dans-des-conditions-encadrees)) |
| Canada | First generic semaglutide (Sevmia) authorised 29 June 2026 ([Health Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/news/2026/06/canada-approves-first-generic-semaglutide-for-weight-loss.html)); the national drug agency recommends public coverage of Wegovy and Zepbound with conditions and lower prices, but plans decide |
| EU | Wegovy pill and the 7.2 mg dose approved July 2026; orforglipron still under review |
| US and UK | Wegovy pill (FDA, December 2025); orforglipron (FDA, April 2026; UK, August 2026) |

**Never buy these online without a prescription.** France's medicines agency, the EMA and Health Canada have all warned about counterfeit pens and pills that contain the wrong substance or none ([ANSM](https://ansm.sante.fr/actualites/alerte-sur-les-risques-associes-a-lachat-sur-internet-daglp-1-contrefaits), [Health Canada](https://recalls-rappels.canada.ca/en/alert-recall/thinking-about-buying-glp-1-drugs-ozempic-or-mounjaro-beware-fake-or-unauthorized)). In Canada, a real product carries an eight-digit DIN.

## Surgery

For severe obesity, surgery gives the largest lasting loss: 25% lower weight 10 years after gastric bypass in the Swedish Obese Subjects study, with fewer deaths than usual care.

| Finding | Numbers | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weight at 10 years, 4,047 people | Gastric bypass -25%, gastroplasty -16%, banding -14%; usual care under 2% | [SOS, Sjöström 2007](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa066254) |
| Deaths over 10.9 years | 101 vs 129; adjusted HR 0.71 | Same |
| Life expectancy | 3.0 years longer than usual care, still 5.5 years shorter than the general population | [Carlsson 2020](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2002449) |

**Who it is for.** International surgical societies recommend it from a BMI of 35 whatever the other conditions, and suggest considering it from 30 with metabolic disease; from 27.5 in Asian populations ([ASMBS and IFSO 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.soard.2022.08.013)). In France, public insurance covers it from a BMI over 40, or over 35 with a complication, from age 18 to 60, after months of diet, activity and psychological support ([ameli](https://www.ameli.fr/assure/sante/themes/surpoids-obesite-adulte/traitement-medicamenteux-chirurgical)). Surgery is a lifelong commitment to follow-up care.

## In your style

The plan is the same for everyone; only the framing changes. Tailoring messages to a person helps a little (r = .074 across 57 studies, [Noar 2007](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.133.4.673)), so treat this as a light touch. Evidence for these specific framings: Early.

| Your setting | Framing that tends to fit | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choleric (outgoing, reactive) | A clear target, a weekly scoreboard, a challenge with a date | All-or-nothing after one bad day |
| Sanguine (outgoing, steady) | Doing it with others: walking partners, classes, shared meals planned ahead | Social eating and drinking that nobody counts |
| Melancholic (reserved, sensitive) | A written plan, tracking, a calm routine | Harsh self-talk after a slip; progress is a trend, not a day |
| Phlegmatic (reserved, steady) | Defaults that remove decisions: the same breakfast, the same walk, groceries on a list | Drift when routines break, on holidays and moves |

No setting changes the science: the deficit, protein, strength training, sleep and patience apply to all four.

## Myths, graded

| Claim | Verdict | What the evidence shows | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Starvation mode stops weight loss" | Mostly false | The slowdown is real but small in controlled trials (72 to 108 kcal a day); men eating half their needs still lost 6 kg in 3 weeks ([Müller 2015](https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.115.109173)) | Good |
| "My metabolism crashed at 40" | False | Energy use adjusted for lean mass is stable from 20 to 60 ([Pontzer 2021](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe5017)) | Strong |
| "Crunches burn belly fat" | False | Six weeks of abdominal training changed no measure of fat ([Vispute 2011](https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e3181fb4a46)) | Some |
| "Skipping breakfast makes you gain" | Not supported | In 13 trials, breakfast eaters ate 260 kcal a day more and weighed 0.44 kg more ([Sievert 2019](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l42)) | Some |
| "Eating late makes you fat" | Unclear | Late eating raised hunger in a lab study ([2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2022.09.007)); a 4-week trial found no difference in weight loss ([2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2022.08.001)) | Early |
| "Fasting beats dieting" | False | No better than ordinary advice in 21 trials ([Cochrane 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD015610.pub2)) | Some |
| "Only carbs (or only fat) matter" | False | Low-carb and low-fat end up level at 12 months ([Johnston 2014](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2014.10397)) | Strong |
| "Fat burners work" | Mostly false | At best 0.7 to 1.1 kg more than placebo; some linked to harm ([NIH ODS](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/WeightLoss-HealthProfessional/)) | Some |
| "Detox teas melt fat" | False | They act as laxatives: the loss is water and gut contents ([BDA](https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/detox-diets.html), [NCCIH](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/detoxes-and-cleanses-what-you-need-to-know)) | No evidence of benefit |

## Gaining weight safely

With a BMI under 18.5, gain gradually: about 300 to 500 extra kcal a day, more protein and strength training, after a doctor has checked why the weight is low ([NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/healthy-weight/advice-for-underweight-adults/)).

1. Eat smaller meals more often, with snacks between.
2. Add energy-dense foods such as cheese, nuts and seeds, and milky drinks between meals.
3. Add protein at every meal.
4. Train with weights or bodyweight so the gain includes muscle.
5. Skip the shortcut of cakes, chocolate and sugary drinks: they add weight without the nutrients.

See a doctor if you lost weight suddenly or without trying, or if you control food to cope with feelings. Being underweight raises the risk of missing vitamins and minerals, a weaker immune system and fragile bones.

## Safety

Talk to a doctor before changing your weight if you have a health condition, take regular medicines, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or are under 18. Get help early if food, weight or exercise start to control your days.

**Warning signs of an eating disorder** ([NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/feelings-symptoms-behaviours/behaviours/eating-disorders/overview/)): spending a lot of time worrying about weight and shape; avoiding social events that involve food; eating very little; making yourself sick or using laxatives after eating; exercising too much; very strict routines around food; feeling cold, tired or dizzy; periods stopping. Others may notice sudden weight loss, lying about eating, or baggy clothes.

**This guide will never:** set targets below the healthy range, promote very fast loss, or sell weight-loss products.
